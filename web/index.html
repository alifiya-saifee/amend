FAQ

Questions

Is my money safe?

The brand's payment is a PayPal authorization hold on the brand's account, not money held by Amend. Money is collected only when both sides approve the release, and only after the delivery check passes. This is a sandbox demo: no real money moves.

How long does PayPal hold the money?

Up to 29 days. Amend collects as soon as the delivery matches, usually within days of posting, so a deal never waits 30–90 days. If a hold gets close to expiry, Amend warns both sides, and can renew it once.

What if the brand cancels?

Amend proposes a fair split from the evidence (draft uploaded, approved, posted) and the creator's own cancellation terms, with the maths shown and a second-opinion check. Both approve; PayPal collects the creator's share and releases the rest.

How is the post checked?

Amend checks the post against the latest confirmed deal: the link, the date, the caption and what was delivered. When the creator's Instagram account is connected, Amend reads the post itself from Instagram (its type, when it went up and the caption); otherwise it uses the details the creator gives, and says so on the check. When the creator's Instagram isn't connected, her details are labelled as hers and the brand confirms them. Stories can't be read for other accounts. Code decides; no money is released until they match.

What if the creator doesn't deliver?

Nothing is released until the delivery matches the latest confirmed deal. Missing items become cards: remove them from the deal (and collect less) or wait for delivery.

Do brands need an account?

No. The creator's link is enough to write a brief, pay with PayPal and confirm changes. Brands can create an account to keep a shortlist and see all their deals.

Which countries and currencies?

Payments are in USD (PayPal doesn't support AED). AED shown at the official peg (3.6725 per USD, UAE Central Bank). PayPal settles in USD in this sandbox flow. The demo is built for the UAE and the GCC.

Is this live?

It's a working demo on PayPal's sandbox. People, brands and numbers are made up and labelled "Demo".

How are creator metrics checked?

Every number shows where it came from: self-reported, read from an insights screenshot (checked by the creator, not verified), verified, or demo, plus how old it is. Engagement and view rate are worked out by Amend, never typed.
