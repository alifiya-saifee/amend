Amend
Money that changes when the truth changes.

Brand deals change. Now the money does too.

Amend is a deal link for creators and brands. The brand pays with PayPal and the money is held, not taken.

When the deal changes in the chat, Amend prices the change from the creator's own list and shows exactly what moves.

Nothing is paid out until both sides confirm and the delivery matches the deal.

How it works

  1. 1
    Send the brand your deal link

    No account for them. They write a brief; the AI drafts the deal from your price list, with the exact words behind every line.

  2. 2
    They pay, PayPal holds it

    The money is reserved, not taken. You start work once it's held.

  3. 3
    Changes are confirmed by both, money moves only when it matches

    An extra Story, a new date, a cancellation: each becomes a card you both confirm. Paid means refund, never cancel.
