Try Amend in 2 minutes

You play both sides of a small made-up deal (capped at $100, PayPal sandbox). Watch the money change when the deal changes.

  1. 1
    Hold

    Open the brand's link and hold the money with a PayPal sandbox buyer account.

  2. 2
    Change

    In the chat, type as the brand: “Add a Story and post Monday”. A change card appears with the ripple and the untouched count.

  3. 3
    Confirm

    Confirm as the creator too. The deal goes to version 2 and a new hold appears for the difference.

  4. 4
    Deliver and get paid

    As the creator, upload a short video and mark it posted. Resolve any gap, then both approve the release.

PayPal sandbox only: no real money. Use a PayPal sandbox personal account to pay (details in the Devpost submission).
